# 03 - 范围边界

状态：已确认整体产品方向；详细行为待 Excel Formula Map 验证。

## In scope (DEC-001)
1. Property information and assumptions.
2. Purchase price, bank value/SPA, deposits, legal/stamp/valuation and other acquisition fees.
3. Mortgage amount, financed fees, MRTA/CRTA, interest, principal, installment, balance and amortization.
4. Renovation breakdown and conditional inclusion.
5. Whole-unit and room-by-room rental scenarios.
6. Rental operating expenses, yield, ROI and monthly cash flow.
7. Monthly investment schedule, appreciation, accumulated cash position and selling scenarios.
8. Investment/export summary.

## Non-negotiable calculation constraint (DEC-002)
- Use `Property Calculation v2.xlsx` as the baseline, including custom formulas, dependencies, condition handling and rounding.
- Do not replace unusual definitions (e.g., ROI or rental yield) with industry-standard versions without explicit approval.
- Any workbook inconsistency is logged, tested and discussed before changes.
- Target parity is defined by a test matrix comparing equivalent inputs and outputs; **not yet verified**.

## Out of scope / not decided
- Live bank rates, property feeds, tax/legal-rate refreshes, multiuser collaboration, login, payments, cloud sync and deployment platform.
- Changes to original workbook formulas without user approval.
- Final UX interactions until wireframes are reviewed.
